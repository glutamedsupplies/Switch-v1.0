# User Manual

## Getting Started

1. Open the Switch app.
2. Continue as guest or sign in with email and password.
3. Register a new account if you do not have one.
4. Guest mode allows browsing, but actions such as orders, chat, and account-specific data may require login.

## Browse Products

1. Open the Home or Shop tab.
2. Scroll through product cards.
3. Use category filters to narrow products.
4. Use search to find products by name or details.
5. Use visual search, where available, to search by product photo.
6. Tap a product card to open product details.

## View Product Details

1. Review images, videos, product description, price, sale price, rating, stock, seller/company, and available variants.
2. Open media previews when needed.
3. Check customer reviews.
4. Tap seller/company identity to view the seller page.
5. Use chat if you need help with the product.

## Add to Cart

1. Open a product.
2. Tap add to cart.
3. Select variant and quantity when prompted.
4. Confirm the selection.
5. Open the cart from the app header.
6. Update quantities, remove items, move items to favorites, or proceed to checkout.

## Direct Buy

1. Open a product.
2. Tap buy/direct buy.
3. Select variant and quantity.
4. Continue to the booking/checkout page.

## Checkout and Booking

1. Start checkout from cart or direct buy.
2. Review the selected products.
3. Enter client name, contact number, and delivery address.
4. Select delivery partner when available.
5. Select payment partner and payment collection option when available.
6. Confirm the order.
7. The app opens the appropriate order tab after successful placement.

## Track Orders

1. Open the Orders tab.
2. Select the relevant stage tab, such as to pay, to prepare, to ship, or to receive.
3. Open an order to view details.
4. Use tracking screens where available.
5. If cancellation is available, submit a cancellation request and wait for admin action.

## Review Products

1. Open an eligible order or product review action.
2. Select a rating.
3. Add a comment.
4. Upload review photos or video if needed.
5. Submit the review.
6. Seller replies appear with the review when provided.

## Chat With Support

1. Open chat from the product detail page, seller page, or chat tab.
2. Send text or media.
3. Use reply, edit, delete, reactions, or product pinning where available.
4. View typing/read state when supported.

## Follow Sellers

1. Open a seller profile.
2. Review seller details, products, rating, and follower count.
3. Tap follow or unfollow.

## Manage Profile

1. Open the Profile tab.
2. View or update profile details where supported.
3. Change password through the password flow if available.
4. Log out to clear the current session and account-specific local stores.

## Guest Mode Limitations

Guest users can browse products, but account-specific actions are limited. Login is recommended for cart persistence, order history, chat, reviews, seller following, and profile management.
