Help Centre

Need help signing in, resetting your password, or accessing your Switch account? Start with these quick guides and support options.

Login Help

Double-check your seller email or employee ID, then make sure your password matches the account you are trying to open.

Password Reset

  • Use `Forgot password?` on the login page.
  • Enter your registered Gmail address.
  • Check your inbox for the 6-digit reset code.

Need More Help?

If you still cannot access your account, contact support so your login details or account status can be checked.
