Discount type
On = percent of total purchase. Off = fixed price off total purchase.
Free shipping
When on, this voucher waives shipping on the order (can be used with or without a purchase discount).
Passive voucher
When on, this voucher becomes available automatically at Place Order once the buyer reaches the minimum spend. Platform rules stay the same (including All platforms).
